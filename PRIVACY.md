# Privacy Policy — Flappy Rain

_Last updated: 31 July 2026_

Flappy Rain does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

## What the game stores

Everything the game remembers is written to your device's local app storage
(via `shared_preferences`) and never leaves it:

- your high score and current rank
- your coin balance and which bird skins you own or have equipped
- your daily-streak counter and the last day you claimed a reward
- today's mission progress and which achievements you've unlocked
- your sound, music, haptics and reduced-motion settings

Deleting the app deletes all of it. There is no account, no login, and no
cloud save.

## What the game does not do

- No analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting SDKs.
- No advertising, and no advertising identifier is read.
- No network requests of any kind — the game runs entirely offline.
- No access to the camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, or files.
- No data is sold or shared with third parties, because none is collected.

## Permissions

The Android build declares no runtime permissions. The iOS build requests
none. The only device features used are the screen, the speaker, and the
haptic engine — all of which you can silence in the in-game Settings panel.

## Children

The game is suitable for all ages and, because it collects nothing, it does
not knowingly or unknowingly collect data from children under 13 (COPPA) or
under 16 (GDPR).
